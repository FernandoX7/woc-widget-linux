## [0.1.0-beta.1] - 2026-07-16

### Added

- Linux Tauri/Svelte dashboard with Overview, `$WOC`, Community, and Settings views.
- Live realm status, honest seven-day local player history, analytics, and export/deletion controls.
- DEX Screener quote metrics and GeckoTerminal OHLCV candles with independent provenance.
- Baseline-safe alerts, quiet hours, cooldowns, hysteresis, mute, and disable controls.
- Cross-desktop StatusNotifierItem tray and native COSMIC panel applet.
- Debian, AppImage, local Flatpak, and separate COSMIC applet packaging definitions.

This is the first packaged beta. Debian and AppImage artifacts are published for testing on
Ubuntu and Pop!_OS; other desktops remain best effort while the compatibility matrix is verified.
